Australian Weather Data Visualisation

Australia’s Rainfall Divide

Rain does not fall evenly across Australia. This visual story explores where rainfall is strongest, how rainfall changes across seasons, and which weather conditions are linked with rain the next day.

1. Where Does Rain Fall Across Australia?

The map shows weather locations across Australia using matched city coordinates. Larger circles represent higher average rainfall, while darker blue circles show locations where rain tomorrow was more common.

2. The Wettest and Driest Locations Show a Clear Divide

Australia’s rainfall pattern is highly uneven. Some coastal and northern locations receive much more rainfall than inland and dry-region locations.

Wettest Australian Weather Locations

Driest Australian Weather Locations

3. Rainfall Changes by Month

Rainfall is not only different by place. It also changes across the year. Darker blue cells show months with higher average rainfall, helping reveal seasonal rainfall patterns.

4. Rainfall Varies Over Time

This chart shows how average rainfall varied across the recorded years. It should be read as rainfall variation within this dataset, not as complete proof of long-term climate change.

5. Rainy Days Are Not Always Heavy Rain

This scatterplot compares how often rain occurs with how much rain falls on average. Larger circles show locations with more heavy rain days.

6. Where Is Rain Tomorrow Most Likely?

This chart ranks the locations where rain was most likely to occur the next day in the historical dataset. It shows likelihood based on past records, not a live forecast.

7. What Weather Conditions Are Linked With Rain Tomorrow?

Humidity and pressure are useful weather signals. They do not prove direct causation by themselves, but they show conditions commonly associated with rain the next day.

Humidity and Rain Tomorrow

Pressure and Rain Tomorrow

8. Wind Direction and Rain Tomorrow

Wind direction can reveal how incoming weather patterns are associated with rain the next day. The chart compares rain tomorrow rates across different wind gust directions.

9. Final Rainfall Profile

The final view groups selected locations into simple rainfall profiles based on rainfall frequency, rainfall amount, and rain tomorrow rate. This summarises the main story: rainfall risk differs strongly depending on where you are in Australia.
